FAQ

What is MIRU?

MIRU (miru.plus) is an interactive romance-story app. You play as yourself, talk with fictional adult characters in your own words, and make choices in every chapter that change relationships, the plot and the ending.

How does MIRU work?

Pick a story, start chapter one and reply however you like. Stories are written by the MIRU team (world, characters, chapters, multiple endings); characters' live replies are generated by AI inside each story's written personalities and safety rules. Your choices are remembered across chapters.

Is MIRU free?

Yes. Every story can be started for free; optional extra scenes use in-app diamonds that can also be earned by playing.

Where can I use MIRU?

In any modern browser at https://miru.plus on phones and computers; an Android app is in testing.

Which languages does MIRU support?

English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Spanish. Stories are translated in full before release rather than translated on the fly.

What kinds of stories are there?

Romance across genres — Mafia Boyfriend, Billionaire & CEO Boyfriend, Vampire Boyfriend, Werewolf Boyfriend, K-pop Idol Boyfriend, Prince & Royal Boyfriend, Hockey & Athlete Boyfriend, Demon & Villain Boyfriend, Professor & Teacher Boyfriend, Bodyguard Boyfriend — plus BL and GL stories.

Is MIRU safe?

All characters are fictional adults; there is no sexually explicit content; intimacy only happens when you clearly agree, and you can stop at any time. Content and AI replies can be reported in the app.

Can I create my own story?

Yes. MIRU's Create tab guides you step by step — idea, characters, conflict, chapters, art and endings — and runs a pre-publish check before your story goes live.
