FAQ

How is MIRU different from general AI chat apps?

General AI chat apps are built around open-ended chat with user-made characters. MIRU is built around written interactive romance stories: each character belongs to a story with a world, a conflict, chapters and multiple endings, and your choices move that story forward.

Does MIRU have long-term memory?

Your choices and key moments are saved with the story, so later chapters and characters respond to what you already did.

Is MIRU free like other AI chat apps?

Yes. Every story can be started for free in your browser; optional extra scenes use diamonds, which you can also earn by playing.
