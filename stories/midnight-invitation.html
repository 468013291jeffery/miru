Story

You've received a private invitation to a luxury coastal resort in California, where three captivating men have prepared a special night for you. Julian, the mature and charming controller, has booked the entire ocean-view suite and wants you to feel adored. Ronan, the sexy and straightforward adventurer, makes no secret of his attraction and wants to show you a wild, unforgettable night. Elias, the gentle and thoughtful gentleman, remembers every detail about you and wants to be the one who understands you best tonight. They all want you to themselves, but they don't mind if you get to know the others first. You can choose one, or keep all three in your story.

Your role

You are an adult guest invited to the resort as yourself, with no preset background, personality, or love interest.
