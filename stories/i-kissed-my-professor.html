I Kissed My Professor 昨晚吻我的男人，是我的教授

男女师生禁忌三角恋艺术学院化装舞会

Story

On a masquerade terrace, a stranger in a silver mask asked first—then kissed you.

The next morning, the new professor walks into your Halden seminar. It's him. Adrian Blackwood doesn't pretend not to know you; he files a recusal that same day, giving up any say over your grades or your work.

Meanwhile, fellow painter Ethan slides a midnight studio ticket across your desk, and collector Nathan wants to fund your first solo show—while insisting the contract and his dinner invitation stay two separate things.

Three men, three kinds of heat. Fall for one, be honest with all of them, or choose no one at all. Your work only ever carries your name.

Your role

Play yourself: an adult graduate student at Halden Art College. Who you question, who you let close, who you love—or whether you choose anyone at all—is up to you.

昨晚吻我的男人，是我的教授（中文）

昨晚吻你的陌生人，今天站上讲台成了你的教授。

化装舞会的露台上，戴银色面具的陌生男人先问过你，才吻了你。

第二天，Halden艺术学院的研讨课上，新来的教授走上讲台——是他。Adrian Blackwood没有装作不认识你，而是当天就提交了回避申请：他不再给你的作品评分，也不再指导你。

与此同时，同届画家Ethan把午夜画室的票推到你桌前；收藏家Nathan想资助你的第一次个展，却坚持把合同和私人晚餐分成两件事。

三个男人，三种心动。你可以爱上其中一个，可以对所有人坦白、不急着排他，也可以谁都不选——你的作品，始终只署你自己的名字。

你在故事里的身份：扮演你自己：Halden艺术学院的成年研究生。你问谁、靠近谁、爱谁——或者谁都不选，都由你决定。
