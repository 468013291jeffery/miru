The System Made Me Love My Killer 系统让我爱凶手

He killed you last time, but the system called it a success—now you're getting close again, just to make him hand over the evidence.

Story

The ring box is still open on the table, and your fiancé slides a glass of water toward you. Your phone says the engagement party is in three days. Last time, he stopped you in the second-floor parlor and pushed you off the terrace—and as everything went dark, the system whispered, "Mission accomplished." Adrian offers to handle every problem for you, Cole leaves a hotel visitor pass on the table, and Ethan sends an installation form asking if the device suits you—each of them knows something you haven't uncovered yet. Only you remember your past life, and now the system is urging you to say yes to a date.

Your role

You're a 28-year-old former investigative journalist now reviewing projects for the foundation your mother founded.

Main characters

Short dark brown hair, gray-blue eyes, a faint scar on his left eyebrow. Sturdy shoulders and back, black shirt, charcoal gray jacket, steel-colored wristwatch. When getting down to business, he turns the documents toward the other person first, confirms the exits but does not block the door.

Ethan

Outside technical consultant for the group, responsible for the terminal support program · 29

Short, slightly curly black hair, light brown eyes, a small silver stud in his right ear. Dark green jacket, gray crewneck shirt, faint old burn marks on his fingers. He spins a pen when joking, and puts the pen down when he truly has to take responsibility.

Adrian

Heir to the medical group, the player's fiancé · 32

Neat dark blond short hair, blue-gray eyes, navy suit, white shirt, silver cufflinks. He keeps a dignified smile, but when a question spirals out of control he pauses to adjust his cuffs.

Julia

Finance director at the foundation, the player's friend · 30

Chestnut-red shoulder-length straight hair, brown eyes, wine-red shirt, black trousers, thin gold-rimmed glasses. She aligns the edges of documents neatly, and when nervous she repeatedly takes her glasses off.
