Crown of Midnight 零点王冠

Condemned to death, you watch your executioner lower his sword as the crown shatters—and four men with old grudges each want to claim you.

RomantasyDramaRoyal CourtDragonsSecret HeirHidden IdentityWhy ChooseEnemies to LoversSlow BurnGenius

Story

You are Lia, a 24-year-old clockmaker born without magic. When the royal crown cracks at your execution and your shackles glow with words only you can read, everything changes. A knight defies orders, a genius mage forgets someone important, a dragon-fire wielder clings to a broken cup, and a blind astrologer cannot promise his visions are true. Each hides secrets, and with seven sunrises before the city's protective magic may vanish, you must decide who to trust—or trust no one at all.

Your role

A 24-year-old clocktower repairer with no magic marks, accused of stealing crown shards and sentenced to death—but you secretly read the strange words on your shackles.

Gray-white curly hair pinned up, wine-red apron, cuffs always dusted with flour; when talk turns serious, she wipes a cup clean and sets it down.

零点王冠（中文）

你被判死刑，处决你的骑士却先放下剑。王冠当众开裂，四个各有旧账的男人都想带你走。可你的命运，凭什么交给他们？

你是24岁修钟匠莉娅，天生不会魔法。王室说你偷了王冠碎片，把你押上审判台。就在剑要落下时，王冠突然开裂，你手上的锁环亮起了只有你认识的字。

想处死你的骑士第一次违抗命令；天才法师正在忘记重要的人；能喷出龙火的男人舍不得扔一只破杯子；能听出誓言破绽的失明占星师，却不敢保证自己说的未来会成真。

他们各自有本事，也各自瞒着事。你可以查证据，和他们掷骰赌一句真话，带谁去龙谷冒险，或邀谁在王宫跳舞。七次日出后，守护全城的魔法可能消失。你是先洗清冤屈，还是留下改变这座城？相信谁，甚至谁也不信，都由你决定。

你在故事里的身份：24岁钟楼修理工莉娅，没有魔纹。处境：被指偷王冠碎片，将受处决。藏着的事：能读锁环上的怪字。
