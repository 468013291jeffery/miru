男女英雄反派多男主悬疑冒险

Story

You restore artifacts at a New York museum. On a late shift, a mysterious golden core awakens inside an object you were repairing.

The city's hero, AEGIS, saves you. Its most wanted villain, NOCTIS, says the foundation behind that hero already knew your name.

Investigator Owen refuses to sign away your freedom. Hacker Nico knows a route without cameras. Four grown men offer four very different answers.

This is not a story about being claimed. You may question, investigate, draw closer, set boundaries, or choose nobody. What happens to the Corona Core is yours to decide.

Your role

Play yourself: an adult artifact restorer at a New York museum. Who you question, who you trust, who you let close—or whether you choose anyone at all—is up to you.
