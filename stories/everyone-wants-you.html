Story

You arrive as the surprise guest on Heartline Island, a fictional dating show. Adrian, the impossibly composed heir, breaks his own rules just to talk to you. Mateo, the man who flirts with everyone, stops laughing when someone else catches your eye. Julian, the quiet one, is the only man asking what you actually want. Then someone tells you your casting file was marked before you arrived. Date anyone, or no one — you are never forced to kiss or commit. 6 episodes, 5 endings.
