Puppy Love House 今天也想黏着你

都市言情合租日常五位男主温馨甜宠悬疑

Story

You move into a beautiful townhouse in Brooklyn, New York, hoping to find your own rhythm in a new life. The moment you open the door, five grown men say at once: "Welcome home."

Mason is as eager as a golden retriever and would happily hug you first. Elliot seems calm outside, yet quietly leaves a light on for you at home. Noah wins your attention with his cooking and his jokes. Theo is shy, but remembers the little things you mention in passing. Jace is always writing songs, and is afraid of goodbyes.

They all want to be close to you, but none of them gets to decide who you hug, kiss, or love. You can date any one of them, or simply stay friends and keep your life for yourself.

But on your first night, you overhear a line: "We promised not to tell her." And an old letter with your name on it has been waiting on the table for a long time.

You decide who to get close to, which truths to ask for, and whether this house can really be called home.

Your role

Play yourself: the new tenant of a Brooklyn townhouse. Who you get close to, which truths you chase, and whether you stay are all up to you.

今天也想黏着你（中文）

搬进布鲁克林的第一晚，五个男人同时说“欢迎回家”——可他们早就知道你的名字。

你搬进纽约布鲁克林一栋漂亮的联排屋，本来只想在新生活里找回一点自己的节奏。开门那一刻，五个成年男人同时说：“欢迎回家。”

Mason热情得像金毛，恨不得先递给你一个拥抱；Elliot外面冷静，回家却悄悄留灯；Noah靠厨艺和玩笑争取你的注意；Theo害羞，却记得你随口说过的小事；Jace总在写歌，偏偏害怕分别。

他们都想和你亲近，却没有人能替你决定抱谁、吻谁、爱谁。你可以和任何一人约会，也可以只做朋友，把生活留给自己。

只是第一晚，你意外听到一句：“我们答应过不告诉她。”而一封写着你名字的旧信，已经在桌上等了很久。

你要决定：靠近哪一个人，问出什么真相，以及这栋房子是否真的可以叫作家。

你在故事里的身份：扮演你自己：刚搬进布鲁克林联排屋的新住客。和谁亲近、追问什么真相、要不要留下，都由你决定。
