Arrest the Man I Loved 我必须亲手逮捕我的前男友

BL刑侦悬疑破镜重圆美漫

Story

Three years ago, he vanished without a trace. No goodbye, no reason—just an old watch that still keeps perfect time.

Now you're a 27-year-old detective in New York. On a stormy night you arrive at a murder scene, and the suspect inside the tape is your ex-boyfriend, Damien Cross.

Damien is quieter than you remember, and more dangerous. He says he didn't kill anyone. He also says someone is faking footage connected to you.

Your colleague Maya takes over the case. Reporter Jonah won't let the story go, a survivor of an old fire refuses to come forward, and a tech company called Vesper seems able to make "what might have happened" look exactly like real video.

You can ask him why he left, decide whether to let him close again, or never forgive him at all. Love can't stand in for evidence—and evidence can't decide whether you still love him.

When a piece of security footage starts showing your face, what do you prove first: who killed a man, or who is manufacturing the truth?

Your role

Play yourself: a 27-year-old male detective in New York. Asking why he left, whether to let him close again, and what to prove first—it's all your call.

我必须亲手逮捕我的前男友（中文）

暴雨夜的谋杀现场，警戒线里的嫌疑人，是三年前不告而别的前男友。

三年前，他消失得干干净净。没有告别，没有理由，只有一块至今还走得很准的旧手表。

现在你是纽约一名27岁的男性刑警。暴雨夜，你赶到谋杀案现场，发现警戒线里的嫌疑人正是你的前男友 Damien Cross。

Damien比记忆里更沉默，也更危险。他说自己没有杀人，还说有人正在伪造与你有关的影像。

你的同事Maya接手了案件。记者Jonah追着线索不放，一名旧火灾幸存者不愿露面，而名为Vesper的科技公司似乎能把“可能发生过的事”做得和录像一样真实。

你可以追问他为什么走、决定是否重新靠近，也可以永远不再原谅。恋爱不能替代证据，证据也不能决定你是否继续爱他。

当一段监控开始呈现你的脸，你要先证明什么：谁杀了人，还是谁在制造真相？

你在故事里的身份：扮演你自己：纽约一名27岁的男性刑警。追问他为什么离开、要不要重新靠近、先证明什么，都由你决定。
