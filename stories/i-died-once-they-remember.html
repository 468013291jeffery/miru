Story

You fell from a Manhattan penthouse on the night of your engagement. Then you wake up seven days earlier. Your fiancé begs you not to marry him. Your most dangerous rival says he buried you in another life. And your quiet bodyguard carries a photograph of a wedding you don't remember. One of them may have killed you. Another may die trying to save you. 30 chapters.
