My Three Roommates Shift at Midnight 我的三位室友，午夜都会变身

You inherit an old New York observatory—and three tenants who change shape when the midnight bell rings.

男女奇幻变身多男主同居悬疑

Story

You are a 27-year-old night-sky photographer who unexpectedly inherits an old observatory in New York. The house is yours—but after midnight it belongs to an ancient pact.

Your three adult tenants each hide a secret: easy-smiling Ren turns into a fox, quietly protective Soren is a snow leopard, and sharp-tongued, soft-hearted Corvin becomes a raven. The Moon Prism that keeps the peace between humans and shifters is inside these walls, and the Eclipse Society wants it.

You are not anyone’s destined master. You are the pact’s keeper—free to negotiate, or to walk away. Who you trust, who you get close to, who you love—or no one at all—is your choice.

Your role

Play as yourself: a 27-year-old night-sky photographer and the observatory’s new owner. Who you trust and who you love—or no one at all—is up to you.
