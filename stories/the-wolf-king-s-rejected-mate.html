Story

A blizzard traps you in the Silver Hollow valley, where Wolf King Kael's moon mark suddenly burns for you. Elders demand a vow by dawn, but you have agreed to nothing. Then Dr. Rowan's wrist glows with the same mark, shattering an ancient law—and the old stone wall cracks open. His sister once refused a bond and was declared dead; now you must uncover the truth, wait safely, or flee, while the mark offers no answers.
