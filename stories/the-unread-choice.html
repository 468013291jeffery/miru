What is The Unread Choice?

A crown, an unfinished song, and a future only you can choose. The Unread Choice is an interactive romance story on MIRU: you play as yourself, talk with the characters and make choices in every chapter that change relationships, the plot and the ending.

How do I play The Unread Choice?

Open MIRU at miru.plus in any browser on your phone or computer, pick The Unread Choice and start the first chapter. No download is needed.
