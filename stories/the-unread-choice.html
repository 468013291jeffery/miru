故事简介

Hayden, a 20-year-old new university student, meets theater student William and musician Alex. An anonymous privacy violation tests trust and consent. Thirty chapters lead to William, Alex, an independent future, or a carefully negotiated open conversation. English dialogue with bilingual chapter and choice labels.
