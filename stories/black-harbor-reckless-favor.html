Story

You come to return a restored oil painting, only to be dragged into a mob's ledger scandal by a forged receipt. Dario calls off his family's pursuit of you, while Remy arrives with a plan to get you out. One is used to giving orders, the other lives to defy him—both want you to trust them first, and both are hiding something they won't easily confess. You can confront them, test their favor, watch them break rules and get jealous for you, or uncover their secrets and refuse to forgive. When the danger passes, who will still want to spend an ordinary day with you, and who earns a next meeting, is yours to find out.

Your role

You are a 25-year-old independent art restorer hired to repair a seascape oil painting.
