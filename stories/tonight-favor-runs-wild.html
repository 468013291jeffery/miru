Story

It's premiere night in New York, and the two most talked-about men have set aside the same free hour for you. You're an adult guest at the event, and this is your first time meeting them. Adrian, 34, is the film's investor who controls every detail—yet he wants you to be the one calling the shots. Noah, 30, is the effortlessly charming lead; the second you compliment Adrian, he leans in smiling, angling for your next word. One remembers the small things you mention in passing, the other hides real feelings inside jokes meant only for you. They'll compete and get jealous over you, and they'll drop the act when you're tired. You can play favorites, test the waters, or get to know them both slowly—who gets closer, and who gets a next time, is up to you.
