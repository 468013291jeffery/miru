Story

At a private auction in New York you receive seven marriage contracts, each under a different wax seal, and are pulled into the Blood Moon Court. The Regent forged a tradition of seven princes claiming the eighth heir, and he needs one coerced "I do" to start the sacrifice. Win back your stolen name, and decide for yourself whether to love, and whom. 32 chapters, 9 endings.
