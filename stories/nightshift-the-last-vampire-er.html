Story

At midnight, New York’s last emergency room for the nightfolk receives a wounded vampire. “Don’t save me,” he whispers. “Save the man in Room Seven.”

Room Seven has been closed for three years. Its patient, Lucian Ash, is awake and speaking—yet his official file says he died long ago.

Elias, the surgeon, wants to keep the doors open. Rowan, the paramedic, keeps people moving. Mira makes medicines with a price; Imani insists that consent means something; and Celeste is investigating the hospital’s impossible records.

Nobody asks you to be a doctor. You can help coordinate the night, verify what you see, keep your distance, or choose whom to trust. Relationships may grow, but saving people is not a romance test.

Then security footage shows someone who looks like you bringing Lucian inside two hours before you arrived.

The bell rings. By sunrise, the city will decide whether the hospital gets to exist at all.

Your role

Play yourself: someone invited to see this night-shift ER for the first time. Help with the shift, verify the evidence, or simply watch from a safe distance—it's your call.

午夜零点，纽约最后一家专门接待夜民的急诊室，收到一位重伤吸血鬼。他抓住医生的袖口，说：“别救我，先去七号病房。”

七号病房的登记已经停用了三年。躺在那里的人叫 Lucian Ash；他清醒地说话，档案却显示他早已死亡。

外科医生 Elias 想保住每个病人，急救员 Rowan 想让每条救援路线保持畅通。药剂师 Mira 负责会付出代价的魔法药剂，权益协调员 Imani 坚持每个人都能说不。调查员 Celeste 正在追查医院异常的记录。

他们没有请你替医生做诊断，而是邀请你决定是否参与夜班协调、协助核验证据，或者只做安全的旁观者。你可以与他们合作、争论、建立友谊，也可以在合适的时候发展爱情。

当你走进监控室，屏幕显示两小时前的录像：一个像你的人，正在把七号病人送进来。可你才刚到。

凌晨的钟声响起。医院收到一条通知：天亮以前，它将被决定是否继续营业。
