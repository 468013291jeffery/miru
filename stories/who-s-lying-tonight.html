Story

Mia has been missing for four days when a photo from her number appears: you, standing among your ex Nolan, rival Jude, and a stranger named Elias—on a night you were never in New York. Now a rooftop party plans to screen this forged memory at midnight. Find the photo's truth and Mia's whereabouts, but you're not there to confirm any relationship. Walk away, ask for help, or stay—every choice is yours.

Your role

You're an adult woman working in New York and Mia's college friend, deciding what to do next and who to trust.
