Story

You're fighting to reopen an old New York theater when five grown men, all younger than you, walk into your life one after another: one who shields you from contempt, one who cooks you dinner, one who dares to admit his mistakes in public, one who's always trying to make you laugh, and one who quietly guards the last door backstage.

Each of them wants to get closer to you—and each of them hid something about the same disputed takeover.

You can kiss, date, walk away, or find the answers on your own. Harder to cross than any age gap is trust.

Your role

Play yourself: someone fighting to reopen an old New York theater. Uncovering the truth, who you get close to, and whether anyone calls you “older”—it's all your call.
