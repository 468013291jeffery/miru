别叫我姐姐 The Younger Man: Five Reasons to Stay

五个比你年轻的男人，都想留在你身边——可他们都对你隐瞒过同一件事。

都市言情年下五位男主悬疑剧院

故事简介

你为一座纽约旧剧院争取重开的机会，五个比你年轻的成年男人却相继闯进你的生活：一个替你挡下轻蔑，一个为你准备晚餐，一个敢向公众说错，一个总想逗你笑，还有一个习惯在幕后守住最后一道门。

他们都想和你更近一点，却都曾在同一桩收购争议中隐瞒事实。

你可以亲吻、约会、转身离开，也可以独自找到答案。比年龄差更难跨越的，是信任。

你在故事里的身份

扮演你自己：为纽约一座老剧院争取重开的人。查清真相、和谁靠近、要不要被叫“姐姐”，都由你决定。

《别叫我姐姐》是 MIRU 上的一部互动剧：你用自己的名字进入故事，和角色对话，在每一章做出选择。不同的选择会改变人物关系、剧情走向和最终结局。所有角色都是虚构的成年人。

The Younger Man: Five Reasons to Stay

Five men younger than you all want to stay by your side—but every one of them hid the same thing from you.

You're fighting to reopen an old New York theater when five grown men, all younger than you, walk into your life one after another: one who shields you from contempt, one who cooks you dinner, one who dares to admit his mistakes in public, one who's always trying to make you laugh, and one who quietly guards the last door backstage.

Each of them wants to get closer to you—and each of them hid something about the same disputed takeover.

You can kiss, date, walk away, or find the answers on your own. Harder to cross than any age gap is trust.

Your role: Play yourself: someone fighting to reopen an old New York theater. Uncovering the truth, who you get close to, and whether anyone calls you “older”—it's all your call.

An interactive story on MIRU: you play as yourself, talk with the characters and make choices in every chapter that change relationships, the plot and the ending. All characters are fictional adults.
