She Ruined My First Show 她毁了我的首秀

She kissed you the night before your debut. She ended your show the next morning.

GL破镜重圆双女主时装职场

Story

Three years ago, on the night before your first New York runway show, you kissed a young director named Sloane Mercer backstage. She said that once it was over, she'd take you for late-night pizza.

The next day, Sloane shut your show down herself. The supplier's failure was pinned on you, and you lost your job, your sponsors, and a chance that should have been yours. She never explained—not publicly, not to you.

Now you're 28, entering the New York fashion competition Threadline with your small studio's work. The director is Sloane. She's more composed than she was three years ago, and harder to trust.

Jules Rivera, the photographer who helped you start over, is here too. She's wanted you for a long time, and you never really answered when she asked you out.

Then Sloane's ex, Evelyn Hart, arrives with old contracts and investor access. An old photo, a dangerous fabric lot called "9F," and a rewritten incident report start pulling the truth about that night—and that kiss—back into the light.

This time, the work is yours. Ask questions, get close, say no, fall for a woman who's ready to love you for real—or choose no one at all.

Your role

Play yourself: a 28-year-old independent fashion designer in New York. Who you question, who you let close, who you love—or whether you choose anyone at all—is up to you.

她毁了我的首秀（中文）

她在你首秀前夜吻了你，第二天亲手叫停了你的秀。

三年前，你的第一场纽约时装秀开幕前夜，你和年轻导演Sloane Mercer在后台接了吻。她说，等明天结束就去吃凌晨的披萨。

第二天，Sloane亲手叫停了你的首秀。随后供应商的责任被媒体推到你身上，你失去工作、赞助和一次本应属于你的机会。她始终没有向你公开解释。

如今你28岁，带着小工作室的作品参加纽约时尚竞技节目《Threadline》。Sloane竟然是负责导演；她比三年前更成熟，也更难让人相信。

陪你重新开始的女性摄影师Jules Rivera也在现场。她早就对你有好感，而你从来没有正式回答过她的约会邀请。

Sloane的前女友Evelyn Hart随后带着旧合约和投资权限出现。旧照片、危险面料批次“9F”、被改写的事故记录，正一步步让三年前那段爱情和首秀真相重新浮上来。

这一次，事业属于你。你可以追问、靠近、拒绝、爱上任何一位愿意认真爱你的女人，也可以谁都不选。

你在故事里的身份：扮演你自己：纽约一名28岁的独立时装设计师。追问、靠近、拒绝，爱上谁或谁都不选，都由你决定。
