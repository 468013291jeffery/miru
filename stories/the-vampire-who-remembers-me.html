那个记得我每一世的吸血鬼 The Vampire Who Remembers Me

同样的七天，他记得九十九次失去。这次，你不必用生命换来明天。

都市哥特时间循环吸血鬼悬疑三位男主五种未来

故事简介

纽约永夜结界中的同一周已经重置九十九次。第一百次，你带回了死亡的片段，手腕上也多了一枚银色断针。

记得一切的吸血鬼、不断做梦的急救医生、记忆受血誓压制的猎人，握着互相矛盾的证词。原始契约却从未要求你的死亡。

核验被篡改的条款、救回正在被城市遗忘的人，并决定谁能与你走向没有倒计时的未来。爱情、友谊与独立，都由你现在的意愿开始。

《那个记得我每一世的吸血鬼》是 MIRU 上的一部互动剧：你用自己的名字进入故事，和角色对话，在每一章做出选择。不同的选择会改变人物关系、剧情走向和最终结局。所有角色都是虚构的成年人。

The Vampire Who Remembers Me

He remembers losing you ninety-nine times in the same seven days. This time, tomorrow need not cost your life.

The same week inside New York’s Eternal Night boundary has reset ninety-nine times. On the hundredth, you retain fragments of a death and discover a silver broken-hand mark on your wrist.

A vampire who remembers everything, an emergency doctor with recurring dreams, and a hunter bound by a blood oath hold conflicting accounts. Yet the original pact never required your death.

Verify the altered terms, rescue people the city is forgetting, and decide who may share a future without a countdown. Love, friendship, and independence begin with what you want now.

Your role: Play yourself as an adult. Your name, attitude, and intimate boundaries are yours to decide.
