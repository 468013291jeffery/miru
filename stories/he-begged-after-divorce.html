Story

You've been divorced from Adrian for three months. The Luma smart mirror's look and interface came from your original designs, meant only for internal testing — never a permanent sale. At the launch, Harper, the creative director who's crushed on him for two years, takes your work as her own while clinging to his arm. Adrian never officially dated her, yet he let her cross the line for far too long. Now a permanent transfer contract you never signed has surfaced, and you want your name back — and the truth about who's responsible. Whether you reconcile is yours to decide.

Your role

You are the original designer of the Luma smart mirror and Adrian's ex-wife, deciding your next move and what this relationship becomes.
