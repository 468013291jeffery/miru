MIRU Interactive Stories

Interactive romance stories where you are the main character: talk to the characters, make choices and reach your own ending.

Browse every MIRU original story — read the premise, meet the characters and see the chapters before you play. Genres include fantasy romance, mystery and twists, survival, royals and billionaires, vampires and werewolves. Every story has multiple endings, and what you say and choose changes the story. Play free in your browser on phone or computer at miru.plus.
