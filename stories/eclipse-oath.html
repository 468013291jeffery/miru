月蚀之誓 Eclipse Oath

三个狼印，一座继承而来的庄园。你可以选择爱情，也可以改写誓约。

狼人奇幻自主选择信任修复

故事简介

你是一位25岁以上的成年女性，刚继承已故姨母留在黑松镇的庄园。你仍是人类，却开始听见山谷古老月井的回声。

暴风雨夜，狼群首领凯尔、被放逐的追踪者罗文和预言者卢西恩先后来到你面前。他们与姨母的往事各有联系，也各自保留着尚未说出的事情。庄园管家玛拉是你可以寻求现实支持的人。

议会称你为“月契者”，声称旧誓约已经替你选好了未来。但印记不是同意，保护也不等于所有权。是否信任、与谁靠近，以及山谷该如何运行，都需要由你亲自判断。

怎么玩

《月蚀之誓》是 MIRU 上的一部互动剧：你用自己的名字进入故事，和角色对话，在每一章做出选择。不同的选择会改变人物关系、剧情走向和最终结局。所有角色都是虚构的成年人。

Eclipse Oath

Three wolf seals, an inherited manor, and an oath you can choose to change.

You are an adult woman of twenty-five or older, newly inheriting your late aunt's manor in Blackpine. You remain human, yet you have begun hearing echoes from the valley's ancient moon well.

On a stormy night, Kael, the pack's leader, Rowan, an exiled tracker, and Lucien, a seer, each come to meet you. Each has ties to your aunt's past and things he has not yet explained. Mara, the manor's caretaker, offers practical support.

The council calls you Moonbound and claims an old oath has already chosen your future. A mark is not consent, and protection is not ownership. Whom to trust, whether to grow closer, and how the valley should be governed remain yours to decide.

Your role: You enter as the adult heir. Your name, words, physical contact, and relationship boundaries are yours to choose.
