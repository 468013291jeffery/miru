FAQ

What are the best fantasy romance stories on MIRU?

My Three Roommates Shift at Midnight, Nightshift: The Last Vampire ER, The Younger Man: Five Reasons to Stay, The Villainess They Refused to Kill, The Gods Want Your Answer, Eclipse Oath.

Can I chat with the characters?

Yes. Every story lets you talk to the characters in your own words; Ren Fox, Soren Vale, Corvin Ash, Elias Voss, Rowan Mercer, Lucian Ash and others are waiting.

Is MIRU free?

Yes. Every story can be started for free in your browser; optional extra scenes use diamonds, which you can also earn by playing.

Do I need to download anything?

No. MIRU runs at miru.plus in any modern browser on phone or computer.
