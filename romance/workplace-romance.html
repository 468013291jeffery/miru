FAQ

What are the best office & workplace romance stories on MIRU?

Letters From Tomorrow, She Ruined My First Show, Arrest the Man I Loved, He Begged After Divorce, Off Stage: Six Idols, One Secret, The City Fears Him, He Fears My Tears.

Can I chat with the characters?

Yes. Every story lets you talk to the characters in your own words; Evan Carter, Nia Brooks, Mrs. Bell, Sloane Mercer, Jules Rivera, Damien Cross and others are waiting.

Is MIRU free?

Yes. Every story can be started for free in your browser; optional extra scenes use diamonds, which you can also earn by playing.

Do I need to download anything?

No. MIRU runs at miru.plus in any modern browser on phone or computer.
