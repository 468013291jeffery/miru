FAQ

What are the best forbidden romance stories on MIRU?

I Kissed My Professor, The Villainess They Refused to Kill, Off Stage: Six Idols, One Secret, Off Limits: The Hockey Boys, The Gods Want Your Answer, Crown of Midnight.

Can I chat with the characters?

Yes. Every story lets you talk to the characters in your own words; Adrian Blackwood, Ethan Brooks, Nathan Sterling, Cassian Vale, Lucien Ash, Kael Thorne and others are waiting.

Is MIRU free?

Yes. Every story can be started for free in your browser; optional extra scenes use diamonds, which you can also earn by playing.

Do I need to download anything?

No. MIRU runs at miru.plus in any modern browser on phone or computer.
