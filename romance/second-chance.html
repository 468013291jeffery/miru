FAQ

What are the best second chance romance stories on MIRU?

Confess Your Darkest Desire, My Fiancé’s Forbidden Brother, Ashes of Us, The Plus-One Pact, The Bride’s Reckoning, She Ruined My First Show.

Can I chat with the characters?

Yes. Every story lets you talk to the characters in your own words; Lucian, Agnes, Nora, Mara, Damian, Ethan and others are waiting.

Is MIRU free?

Yes. Every story can be started for free in your browser; optional extra scenes use diamonds, which you can also earn by playing.

Do I need to download anything?

No. MIRU runs at miru.plus in any modern browser on phone or computer.
