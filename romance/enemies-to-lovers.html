FAQ

What are the best enemies to lovers stories on MIRU?

Who's Lying Tonight?, Off Limits: The Hockey Boys, Everyone Wants You, I Died Once. They Remember., Northlight Still Burns, Crown of Midnight.

Can I chat with the characters?

Yes. Every story lets you talk to the characters in your own words; Nolan, Jude, Elias, Alexander, Jace, Ethan and others are waiting.

Is MIRU free?

Yes. Every story can be started for free in your browser; optional extra scenes use diamonds, which you can also earn by playing.

Do I need to download anything?

No. MIRU runs at miru.plus in any modern browser on phone or computer.
