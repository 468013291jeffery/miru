FAQ

What are the best love triangle & reverse harem stories on MIRU?

The Villain Kissed Me First, I Kissed My Professor, OFF CAMERA, He Begged After Divorce, The Last Light Inn, Everyone Wants You.

Can I chat with the characters?

Yes. Every story lets you talk to the characters in your own words; Adrian Cross, Lucien Vale, Owen Hart, Nico Reyes, Adrian Blackwood, Ethan Brooks and others are waiting.

Is MIRU free?

Yes. Every story can be started for free in your browser; optional extra scenes use diamonds, which you can also earn by playing.

Do I need to download anything?

No. MIRU runs at miru.plus in any modern browser on phone or computer.
