Lena：“I know. That's why you should read the last page.”

场3｜维修。Finn想留下，却希望Lena停止远航计划。
Lena：“Coming back doesn't make you the destination.”
Finn：“I don't know how to stay without asking you to.”
选择共同算成本／用怀旧说服，后一选择使约会延后。

场4｜买家到港。Lena选择继续自己计划，Finn可以卖船、改成合作经营或另找工作。
Finn：“Which choice gets me you?”
Lena：“The one where you stop asking that.”

场5｜最后一次试航。两人谈新的联系方式和边界。
Finn：“Can I call when you leave?”
Lena：“If you have a life to tell me about.”
Finn：“I'm working on that.”
