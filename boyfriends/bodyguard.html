Bodyguard Boyfriend

Bodyguard Boyfriend stories you play as yourself on MIRU.

Each story is an interactive romance: you talk to the characters in your own words and make a choice in every chapter that changes the relationship, the plot and the ending. Every love interest is a fictional adult who asks before getting close and stops when you say no.

FAQ

What are the best bodyguard boyfriend stories on MIRU?

The Younger Man: Five Reasons to Stay, Off Stage: Six Idols, One Secret, The Gods Want Your Answer, The Last Light Inn, I Died Once. They Remember., Crown of Midnight.

Can I chat with the characters?

Yes. Every story lets you talk to the characters in your own words; Eli Hart, Miles Bennett, Rowan Blake, Caleb Foster, Asher Quinn, Seo-jun and others are waiting.

Is MIRU free?

Yes. Every story can be started for free in your browser; optional extra scenes use diamonds, which you can also earn by playing.

Do I need to download anything?

No. MIRU runs at miru.plus in any modern browser on phone or computer.
