你：“You picked a bad seat.”
陆识野：“Same number.”
旁白：You look down. Your seat is 07. The seat across from him is also 07. Ten years, and it's still 07/07.
你：“Still with the 07 thing?”
陆识野：“Still two knocks?”
旁白：You knock twice on the table. He knocks back twice. Ten years, and the signal still matches.
