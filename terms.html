MIRU 用户协议与社区规范

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 22 日。注册或使用 MIRU 即表示你同意本协议。

1. 服务内容

MIRU 是互动剧与 AI 角色聊天应用。剧中角色的回复由人工智能生成，可能不准确，不代表 MIRU 的观点。所有角色均为虚构的成年人。

2. 年龄要求

你必须年满 17 周岁才能使用 MIRU。

3. 对违规内容零容忍

MIRU 对令人反感的内容和辱骂行为零容忍。你不得发布、上传或诱导 AI 生成以下内容：

  • 涉及未成年人的任何性内容，或将角色描绘为未成年人的性内容；
  • 露骨色情内容；
  • 仇恨、歧视、骚扰、威胁、霸凌他人；
  • 鼓励自残、自杀或现实中的暴力；
  • 违法活动、毒品交易、诈骗；
  • 侵犯他人版权、肖像权或隐私（包括他人的真实个人信息）；
  • 冒充真实人物、垃圾信息或广告。

违反规定的内容会被删除，账号会被警告、限制功能或永久封禁。

4. 用户发布的内容（UGC）

  • 你发布到社区的剧本、上传到素材库的图片、音乐、视频，发布前会经过自动安全审查；上传的素材在公开前须经人工审核。
  • 你对自己发布的内容负责，并保证拥有相应权利。
  • 你授予 MIRU 在服务内展示、分发这些内容的非独占许可。你可以随时删除自己的作品。

5. 举报与拉黑

  • 在作品页、聊天菜单（⋯）和素材库中，都可以举报作品、作者、素材或 AI 生成的回复。
  • 你可以拉黑任意作者，拉黑后你将不再看到 TA 的任何作品。在「我的 → 安全与举报」中可以查看和解除拉黑。
  • 我们会在 24 小时内处理举报：删除违规内容，并对发布者采取封禁等措施。被多人举报的内容会在审核前自动下架。

6. 虚拟货币

钻石为服务内虚拟物品，不能兑换现金，不可转让。

7. 联系我们

在 App 内「我的 → 安全与举报 → 联系我们」提交，我们会在 24 小时内回复。

MIRU Terms of Use & Community Guidelines

Effective October 22, 2026. By creating an account or using MIRU you agree to these terms.

1. The service

MIRU is an interactive-drama and AI character chat app. Characters' replies are generated by AI, may be inaccurate, and do not represent MIRU's views. All characters are fictional adults.

2. Age

You must be at least 17 years old to use MIRU.

3. Zero tolerance for objectionable content

MIRU has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. You may not post, upload, or prompt the AI to produce:

  • any sexual content involving minors, or sexual content depicting characters as minors;
  • sexually explicit content;
  • hate, discrimination, harassment, threats or bullying;
  • encouragement of self-harm, suicide or real-world violence;
  • illegal activity, drug dealing or scams;
  • content that infringes copyright, likeness or privacy (including other people's real personal information);
  • impersonation of real people, spam or advertising.

Violating content is removed, and accounts are warned, restricted or permanently banned.

4. User-generated content

  • Stories you publish to the community and images, music or video you upload to the library pass an automatic safety review before publishing; uploads are reviewed by a person before they become public.
  • You are responsible for what you publish and confirm you have the rights to it.
  • You grant MIRU a non-exclusive license to display and distribute it within the service. You can delete your works at any time.

5. Reporting and blocking

  • You can report a story, its author, a library item or an AI reply from the story page, the chat menu (⋯) and the library.
  • You can block any author; you will no longer see any of their works. Manage blocked authors in Me → Safety & reporting.
  • We act on reports within 24 hours: violating content is removed and the person who posted it is banned. Content reported by several people is hidden automatically until it is reviewed.

6. Virtual currency

Diamonds are virtual items for use in the service only. They have no cash value and cannot be transferred.

7. Contact

Use Me → Safety & reporting → Contact us in the app. We reply within 24 hours.
