# M110 · Season Two / Post-ending companion scene pack

这不是追加章节，也不自动替玩家决定恋爱关系。只有12章结局后，玩家自由明确点名某男主并表示愿意发展关系，且工作回避规则落实后，才开放对应的双人亲密场景。友情玩家也可以访问非恋爱版本。每位男主有三段长线生活节拍；每一段都可自由聊天，必须由玩家主动点击下一场。以下角色台词是**场景开场的固定引子**，不是玩家选择后的固定回应；玩家回复后的回应由角色AI生成。

## Alexander Knight · 从控制到平等
### A1 战术室 / Strategy room（新赛季第1周）
- 人物：Alexander把队长袖标放在桌上，另一只手拿着两杯咖啡。/ He places his captain's armband on the table and holds two coffees.
- Alexander: "I used to confuse protecting someone with making decisions for them."
- Alexander: "Want to tell me what I should have asked you back then?"
- 玩家自由聊：边界、歉意、工作和私人生活；不判定恋爱自动升级。
### A2 客场酒店大堂 / Hotel lobby（第3周）
- 他冒雪赶来，但先问你是否方便见面。/ He arrives through the snow and asks whether this is a good moment.
- Alexander: "I don't need you to miss a deadline just because I miss you."
- Alexander: "Would a ten-minute coffee work?"
### A3 青训球场 / Youth rink（第6周）
- 他把小队员的头盔扣好，转身询问你对公益计划的意见。/ He fastens a young player's helmet, then asks for your input.
- Alexander: "You were right. A team isn't a reason to silence anyone."
- Alexander: "Can we build something together without losing our own lives?"

## Jace Walker · 从流量到尊重
### J1 客队训练馆外 / Visiting practice rink（第1周）
- 他把手机屏幕朝下放在长椅上。/ He places his phone face down.
- Jace: "No cameras. No stunt. Just me asking how your day went."
### J2 城市热狗摊 / Hot dog stand（第3周）
- 他把刚买的两杯热巧克力放在桌上，不伸手碰你。/ He sets down two hot chocolates without reaching for you.
- Jace: "If I do something stupid again, tell me. I'll actually listen."
### J3 旧球场 / Neighborhood rink（第6周）
- 他帮孩子们练习射门，输给一个十岁的守门员。/ He loses a shootout to a ten-year-old goalie.
- Jace: "The kid earned that win. I kind of love being bad at something."

## Ethan Brooks · 学会被照顾
### E1 青训活动后的烘焙店 / Bakery（第1周）
- 他坦然给你看医生允许的复健进度表。/ He shows you the rehabilitation calendar cleared by his physician.
- Ethan: "I'm okay saying I need to rest now."
- Ethan: "Would you rather talk or just share a cinnamon roll?"
### E2 康复中心休息区 / Rehab lounge（第3周）
- 他没有隐瞒疼痛，也没有要求你替代医生。/ He reports symptoms honestly and makes no request for you to replace medical care.
- Ethan: "You don't have to fix it. Being here is enough, if you want to be."
### E3 雨夜厨房 / Kitchen（第6周）
- 他烤坏一盘曲奇，自己先笑了。/ He burns a tray of cookies and laughs at himself.
- Ethan: "Turns out I can survive not being perfect."

## Damon Steele · 从交锋到信任
### D1 德比比赛后门 / Arena back exit（第1周）
- 他脱下客队外套，递给你一张普通咖啡馆地址。/ He removes his rival-team jacket and offers a café address.
- Damon: "Not a negotiation. You can say no and I'll still mean it."
### D2 拳馆休息区 / Boxing gym（第3周）
- 他让你先挑选想看的训练，而不是安排你的时间。/ He lets you choose whether to watch drills.
- Damon: "I can compete with them on the ice. Not for ownership of you."
### D3 城市夜跑终点 / Riverside run（第6周）
- 他停在路灯下，等你决定要不要继续散步。/ He stops under a lamp and waits for your decision.
- Damon: "I used to think every question needed a winner. This one doesn't."

## Ryan Cole · 从愧疚到重新开始
### R1 安全公益基金晚宴后 / Charity entrance（第1周）
- 他把调查文件锁回办公室，才走向你。/ He files away the investigation papers before approaching you.
- Ryan: "I owe the people I hurt an honest account. You don't owe me forgiveness."
### R2 老电影放映室 / Old screening room（第3周）
- 他主动承认自己会害怕复发，却不让你的回应承担他的情绪。/ He talks about his fears without making you responsible for them.
- Ryan: "I want a life that isn't a second trial of the same mistake."
### R3 训练营开幕日 / Training camp（第6周）
- 他把第一块冰面留给新来的孩子。/ He lets the youngest players take the first shift.
- Ryan: "I thought my best years were over. Turns out I was measuring the wrong thing."

## 接入条件 / runtime gates
- `romance_target` 必须为五个之一，且 `romance_consent = true`；若仅表达好感但未明确意愿，角色先澄清，不自动进入恋爱路线。
- `career_conflict_resolved = true` 后开放恋爱版；尚未解决时只有友情及公共场合约会前的相处片段。
- 玩家可以随时停止、改为友情、保持单身；不倒扣关系。
- 离线时无扣分；后续更新只以用户当时明确的选择和已发生事件为基础。
