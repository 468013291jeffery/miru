# My Fiancé’s Forbidden Brother

## Story card
18+ adult female audience, US English, 16 chapters × 3 scenes; dark romance, steamy (non-explicit), angst.

## Homepage
Twelve hours before your wedding, you catch Ethan kissing another woman at the rehearsal. He sees you and smiles. ‘Going to my brother? You've wanted to for a while.’

You're a 28-year-old independent designer about to marry into New York's Blackwood family. With an earring the woman deliberately dropped in your hand, you knock on the door of your fiancé's older brother. Damian, 35, is the family's controlled, formidable investment director. Three years ago, you met under false names during a storm and talked until dawn in a hospital corridor. He still has the button you lent him.

Ethan, 32, is charming and terrified of losing to Damian. Eleanor, the family's matriarch, hands you premarital papers that list designs you never agreed to sell. Then Damian produces a photograph from that rainy night. On the back, in Ethan's handwriting: ‘If she falls for my brother, tell her about that night.’

Tomorrow, everyone expects you to say I do. Tonight, you can question either man or stop the wedding yourself. Are they fighting for you, or were you written into a deal years ago?

## Character and voice anchors
- Player: 28, independent New York fashion designer; default shoulder-length dark brown hair, dark brown eyes, tailored ivory blazer or wedding dress; customizable.
- Damian Blackwood: 35, broad shoulders, short dark brown hair, grey eyes, charcoal three-piece suit, one missing left cufflink; controlled low voice.
- Ethan Blackwood: 32, light brown hair, amber eyes, elegant ivory shirt and fitted dark suit, silver tie clip; quick, charming, nervous humor.
- Eleanor: 59, silver-grey ear-length bob, pearl earrings, immaculate dark formal attire; precise, courteous voice.
- Mia: 29, short dark hair, practical paint-marked work clothes, alert eyes; quick pragmatic delivery.
- Claire: 30, long blonde hair, gold satin dress, confident but irritated expression; direct delivery.

## Author-only truth and reveal schedule
Damian and the player met under aliases during a real emergency three years ago. The family later tied the marriage to a studio-control deal; Damian cast the deciding vote. Ethan staged the kiss with Claire to manipulate the player. Eleanor exploited the wedding and its administrative system to leak images and forge design rights. Reveal Damian’s vote at chapter 6, the photo export at 7, Claire’s contract at 8, Eleanor’s secretary at 10, forged license at 11 and the full evidence at 14. Neither brother’s apology erases responsibility. The player retains control of her career and romance.

## World and player control
All characters are fictional adults in a contemporary setting. The player chooses whether to question, cooperate, refuse, or leave. No relationship choice settles a legal dispute. Hidden branches depend on actual player intent, not silence. C-role characters are not romance options.
