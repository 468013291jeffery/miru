# My Fiancé’s Forbidden Brother

## Player-facing introduction — only this section belongs on the homepage
Twelve hours before your wedding, you catch Ethan kissing another woman at the rehearsal. He sees you and smiles. ‘Going to my brother? You've wanted to for a while.’

You're a 28-year-old independent designer about to marry into New York's Blackwood family. With an earring the woman deliberately dropped in your hand, you knock on the door of your fiancé's older brother. Damian, 35, is the family's controlled, formidable investment director. Three years ago, you met under false names during a storm and talked until dawn in a hospital corridor. He still has the button you lent him.

Ethan, 32, is charming and terrified of losing to Damian. Eleanor, the family's matriarch, hands you premarital papers that list designs you never agreed to sell. Then Damian produces a photograph from that rainy night. On the back, in Ethan's handwriting: ‘If she falls for my brother, tell her about that night.’

Tomorrow, everyone expects you to say I do. Tonight, you can question either man or stop the wedding yourself. Are they fighting for you, or were you written into a deal years ago?

## Story card
A fictional contemporary New York romantic suspense for adult women aged 18 and over. Sixteen chapters, three scenes each. Family power, sibling rivalry, original work and forbidden intimacy; emotionally intense, non-explicit. All characters are adults.

## Author-only truth and reveal schedule — never publish as the opening
The storm rescue and meeting under aliases three years ago were real; no hidden coercive encounter occurred. The family later linked marriage to studio control. Damian voted yes out of fear of losing power and remained silent. Administration accessed the photo before Ethan proposed; Ethan knew they had met. He paid Claire to stage the kiss and provoke jealousy. Eleanor exploited the opening for authorization and media manipulation, using a document without the player's genuine signature and freezing studio funds in retaliation.
The vote is revealed in 6, photo export in 7, Claire's contract in 8, secretary's device in 10 and forged license in 11. After the marriage bargain in 12 and separate responsibility in 13, evidence converges in 14. The missing twenty seconds remain an investigative issue; no game result restores them as a fact. Apologies and romance in 13–16 do not cancel rights proceedings.

## Performance and knowledge boundaries
NPCs answer according to their current evidence and relationship stage. Never prewrite the player's attraction, forgiveness or consent. Distinguish Damian's restrained short clauses, Ethan's quick failing jokes, Eleanor's courteous conditions and Mia's practical work focus. Surveillance, threats, evidence destruction, perjury and uninvited contact are not romantic virtues.
