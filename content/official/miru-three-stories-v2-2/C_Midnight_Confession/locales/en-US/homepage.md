# Confess Your Darkest Desire

At midnight, inside a confessional that has been sealed for seven years, a man describes the clothes your missing mother wore on her last known day. ‘A blue coat. One button missing.’

You're a 29-year-old historical archive editor. Your mother Mara vanished from the abbey seven years ago, and everyone insisted she'd left willingly. Now your grandmother's will brings you back to the valley with a strange condition: stay seven nights to receive the inheritance. Lucian, 36, once kept the abbey's records and left religious office seven years ago. He offers you Mara's rosary and a basement key. ‘I opened the door for her. I saw them lock it from the outside.’

Agnes, the administrator, welcomes you to stay. Nora, an independent investigator, warns that medical records have been altered. Lucian wants to be close, but he stops before touching you. His attraction is visible; so is the secret he cannot bring himself to tell.

You can stay to investigate or leave and search from outside. Before you go, you find a portrait of yourself at nine years old on the wall. Someone included you in their plan when your mother vanished. The man who wants to kiss you has been keeping a secret. Whose?
