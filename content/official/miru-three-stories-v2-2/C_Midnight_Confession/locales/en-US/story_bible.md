# Confess Your Darkest Desire

## Story card
18+ adult female audience, US English, 16 chapters × 3 scenes; dark romance, steamy (non-explicit), angst.

## Homepage
At midnight, inside a confessional that has been sealed for seven years, a man describes the clothes your missing mother wore on her last known day. ‘A blue coat. One button missing.’

You're a 29-year-old historical archive editor. Your mother Mara vanished from the abbey seven years ago, and everyone insisted she'd left willingly. Now your grandmother's will brings you back to the valley with a strange condition: stay seven nights to receive the inheritance. Lucian, 36, once kept the abbey's records and left religious office seven years ago. He offers you Mara's rosary and a basement key. ‘I opened the door for her. I saw them lock it from the outside.’

Agnes, the administrator, welcomes you to stay. Nora, an independent investigator, warns that medical records have been altered. Lucian wants to be close, but he stops before touching you. His attraction is visible; so is the secret he cannot bring himself to tell.

You can stay to investigate or leave and search from outside. Before you go, you find a portrait of yourself at nine years old on the wall. Someone included you in their plan when your mother vanished. The man who wants to kiss you has been keeping a secret. Whose?

## Character and voice anchors
- Player: 29, historical archive editor; default dark chestnut hair, dark wool coat, leather folder and old photograph; customizable.
- Lucian Vale: 36, black hair and dark eyes, black high-neck coat, faint scar on back of hand, old badge; soft slow voice; former records keeper, no current clergy authority.
- Agnes: 62, silver hair in a bun, modest dark suit, key ring; calm precise voice.
- Nora: 34, short hair, khaki trench coat, practical bag; concrete, orderly speech.
- Mara: 53, dark hair streaked with silver, old blue coat, red childhood scarf; restrained, hesitant but independent voice.

## Author-only truth and reveal schedule
Agnes’s private care network illegally restricted Mara seven years ago. Lucian saw the door locked from outside and later signed a false discharge certificate. Mara escaped but was kept under threats and false guardianship. Agnes inserted the seven-night condition to control people seeking records; it was not part of the original will. The portrait was painted by Mara, not supernatural. Reveal the footage in chapter 4, letters in 5, payment in 7, will in 8, living voice in 9, false certificate in 12, Mara in 13, public accountability in 14. Lucian has no current religious authority and romance never absolves him.

## World and player control
All characters are fictional adults in a contemporary setting. The player chooses whether to question, cooperate, refuse, or leave. No relationship choice settles a legal dispute. Hidden branches depend on actual player intent, not silence. C-role characters are not romance options.
