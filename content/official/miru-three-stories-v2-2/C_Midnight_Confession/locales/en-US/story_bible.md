# Midnight Confession — Story Bible V2.2
Adult gothic mystery and romance, 16 chapters and 48 scenes. Themes: desire, culpability, accountability, and a missing mother. The executable text is this locale's script.md.

## 01｜Player-facing opening
At midnight, in a confessional sealed for seven years, a man describes your missing mother's blue coat and torn-off button.
You are a 29-year-old historical archive editor. Mara disappeared from this abbey seven years ago; everyone said she left voluntarily. Your grandmother's will brings you back to the valley with a strange requirement: stay seven nights to inherit.
Lucian, 36, is a former records keeper who left the clergy seven years ago. He has no current religious authority or control over you. He offers your mother's rosary and the basement key. His attraction is visible, but he stops before touching you. There is also a guilt he cannot yet explain.
Administrator Agnes welcomes your stay. Independent investigator Nora suspects altered medical records. You may stay or investigate from outside. Before leaving, you discover a painting of yourself aged nine. Why were you included in this secret?

## 03｜Private truth and reveal schedule
1. Mara did not leave voluntarily. The abbey and private-care network restricted her liberty seven years ago.
2. Lucian saw the external lock without stopping it, handled payments, and knowingly signed a false discharge while she could not leave.
3. After escape, financial pressure, false guardianship, and threats prevented safe contact. She did not choose to abandon her daughter.
4. Agnes added the seven-night condition outside the original will. It is no lawful inheritance obligation, but time to assess visitors' investigative reach.
5. Seven letters were intercepted. Mara hid the childhood painting with correspondence: no prophecy or time travel.
6. Reveal footage in 4, letters in 5, payment in 7, false condition in 8, recent recording in 9, deliberate false certification in 12, reunion and payment links in 13, investigative progress in 14.
7. Proceedings remain open in 16. Lucian's accountability continues; romance provides no exemption.

## 04｜Relationships, branches, games, and continuity
Each chapter's three choices carry into corresponding IF blocks next chapter. The player states their own wishes; silence and games imply no consent. Protect boundaries particularly in chapters 3, 6, 10, and 13.
Two optional games per chapter, 32 total: finding an envelope, matching memory icons, sliding numbered 📷 tiles, and repeating four-color lights. Use the actual UI; never claim it reconstructs real evidence. Win, lose, and skip all continue, creating no evidence, trust, intimacy, or forgiveness.
Complete endings are equal romance with ongoing accountability, friendship with boundaries, or an independent future ending private contact. Romance is not a superior reward.
Later companionship can include archive exhibitions, reading, Mara's birthday, and spring in the valley. Remember boundaries and unfinished proceedings, without resetting the conspiracy or confinement. Never penalize absence.
