# The Devil Who Knows My Name

At two in the morning, you slam a detention order onto the desk of New York crime boss Roman Moretti. His signature is right there. ‘Did you kill my brother?’

Roman moves the gun away. ‘I signed the detention order. You have every right to find out who wanted him dead.’ Three months ago, your brother Luca was reported killed in a warehouse fire. You're a 27-year-old documentary photographer, and the police timeline doesn't add up. You came looking for a confession. Instead, Roman remembers how you take your coffee and a secret code from your childhood.

Roman, 36, is dangerous, quiet, and accustomed to control. He doesn't hide his attraction, but that doesn't erase his wrongdoing. Nadia, an independent source with access to old records, warns that his kindness might be part of a plan.

The club goes dark. Roman puts a key to the exit in your hand and stays behind. When the emergency screen lights up, your name is at the top of a bounty list. And Roman himself bought the contract. Is he trying to save you, use you, or make you fall for the last man you should trust?
