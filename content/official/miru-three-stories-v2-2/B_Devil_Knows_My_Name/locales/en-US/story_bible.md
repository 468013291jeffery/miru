# The Devil Who Knows My Name

## Story card
18+ adult female audience, US English, 16 chapters × 3 scenes; dark romance, steamy (non-explicit), angst.

## Homepage
At two in the morning, you slam a detention order onto the desk of New York crime boss Roman Moretti. His signature is right there. ‘Did you kill my brother?’

Roman moves the gun away. ‘I signed the detention order. You have every right to find out who wanted him dead.’ Three months ago, your brother Luca was reported killed in a warehouse fire. You're a 27-year-old documentary photographer, and the police timeline doesn't add up. You came looking for a confession. Instead, Roman remembers how you take your coffee and a secret code from your childhood.

Roman, 36, is dangerous, quiet, and accustomed to control. He doesn't hide his attraction, but that doesn't erase his wrongdoing. Nadia, an independent source with access to old records, warns that his kindness might be part of a plan.

The club goes dark. Roman puts a key to the exit in your hand and stays behind. When the emergency screen lights up, your name is at the top of a bounty list. And Roman himself bought the contract. Is he trying to save you, use you, or make you fall for the last man you should trust?

## Character and voice anchors
- Player: 27, documentary photographer; default shoulder-length chestnut hair, neutral dark coat, black camera and memory cards; customizable.
- Roman Moretti: 36, short black hair, old scar on left eyebrow, dark shirt, tall solid build, understated watch; low clipped voice.
- Luca: 31, dark brown curls, old brow scar, worn ring; fast speech and evasive humor.
- Nadia: 29, short black curls, leather jacket, pocket recorder; crisp factual speech.
- Independent lawyer: 40, black rectangular glasses, neat suit; composed professional voice.

## Author-only truth and reveal schedule
Luca staged his death and used his sister to draw Roman out. Roman signed a detention order but not the lethal order; he destroyed evidence and ordered surveillance. Chapter 3 exposes destruction, chapter 6 surveillance, chapter 7 evidence that Luca is alive, chapter 8 his appearance, chapter 9 Roman’s original approach, chapter 11 Luca’s bait plan, chapters 12–14 independent corroboration and accountability. Roman’s criminal responsibility continues even if the player wants future contact. Nadia is an independent source, not Roman’s employee or a romance option.

## World and player control
All characters are fictional adults in a contemporary setting. The player chooses whether to question, cooperate, refuse, or leave. No relationship choice settles a legal dispute. Hidden branches depend on actual player intent, not silence. C-role characters are not romance options.
