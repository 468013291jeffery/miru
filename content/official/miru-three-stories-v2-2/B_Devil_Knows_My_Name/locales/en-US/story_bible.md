# The Devil Who Knows My Name — Complete Production Bible

You are a 27-year-old independent documentary photographer in New York. Three months ago your older brother Luca was reported dead in a warehouse fire. The chronology does not fit. You enter Roman Moretti's private club carrying the detention order bearing his signature. You can demand answers, examine the original, ask independent people to help, or leave. The production-only information below must not be inserted into this opening.

## Format and point of view
Adult noir romance and investigation, 16 chapters with three scenes each. Second person from an adult woman’s perspective. Preserve questions, refusals, postponement and independent evidence routes. Never narrate the player's romantic consent, forgiveness or settled feelings for her. Assistance, listening, dancing, intimacy and future contact are separate choices. This is a fictional identity; do not import the real user's address, occupation, family or relationships.

## Production-only truth and reveal order
Detention and execution are different orders. Chapter 3 proves the red stamp was added later and establishes Roman's separate destruction of evidence. The chapter 4 dock photo is four days AFTER the death notification; the chapter 5 intake is three weeks AFTER the police report. Chapter 7 combines footage from one month earlier and audio recorded two days earlier. Luca appears in 8. Roman admits both his instrumental approach and subsequent genuine feelings in 9. His written objections in 10 do not excuse tolerating the laundering network. Chapter 11 proves Luca used the player as bait. Chapter 12 independently verifies the other senior figure's lethal order and Roman's cover-up. Do not invent a personal name for that issuer. Chapters 13–15 move through testimony, attempted arson, independent copies and formal receipt; no vigilante killing. The dance-floor image during legal-aid testimony is a recollection. Chapter 16 takes place months later: the letter gives a case update and a practical privacy promise, without demanding a reply or promising immediate reunion.

## Branch and mini-game behavior
Each chapter keeps one GOAL, two DONE criteria and A/B/C consequences. The next chapter replays the exact prior selected consequence through its flag. No branch is a romantic punishment. Two mini-games per chapter, 32 total, are embedded in practical preparation or waiting. Match actual icon UI: a sliding emoji puzzle is not real forensic restoration, lights are not a genuine password, and card matching does not authenticate feelings. Win, lose and skip all continue without new story evidence, relationship increments or consent. The six concrete investigative prompts remain separate from the mini-games. Preserve custody, dates and independent channels.
